The first release of Pesa Ledger: a household budget that runs on your own computer.

## Install

Download the zip for your computer below, unzip it, and double-click **Install Pesa Ledger**. No administrator
rights are needed. The first time, macOS or Windows warns that the installer isn't registered; *Read me first.txt* in
the zip, and the [getting-started guide](https://github.com/ttemu34/pesa-ledger/blob/main/docs/GETTING_STARTED.md),
explain how to continue.

- **Mac:** `Pesa-Ledger-0.1.0-mac.zip` (Apple silicon and Intel)
- **Windows:** `Pesa-Ledger-0.1.0-windows.zip` (64-bit Windows 10 or 11)

To check a download, compare its SHA-256 with the `.sha256` file beside it: `shasum -a 256 <zip>` on a Mac,
`Get-FileHash <zip>` on Windows.

## What's in it

- **Home:** what's left to spend this month, where spending is heading by month end, last month in review, budget
  vs spent by category, and what's worth a look.
- **Budget:** one month at a time, a usual amount per category, and amounts for one month only.
- **Spending:** add expenses as they happen, or type a month's totals; filters for over budget and not recorded.
- **More:** year and two-year comparisons, health checks, net worth, goals (with item lists), sinking funds and
  allocation rules.
- Pay cycles, one-off categories, other currencies at live rates, CSV import and export, daily backups, undo, light
  and dark themes, and the menu at the side or across the top.

Your figures stay in `.pesa-ledger` in your home folder. Nothing is sent anywhere except your main currency's code,
to fetch exchange rates. See [SECURITY.md](https://github.com/ttemu34/pesa-ledger/blob/main/SECURITY.md).
